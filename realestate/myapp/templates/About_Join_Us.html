About Our Team

In 1997, a small group of real estate auctioneers across the country combined their industry experience and local expertise to bring together property buyers and sellers nationwide. Today, more than 20 years later, Tranzon has grown into one of the most experienced -- and most trusted -- names in the world of professional real estate auctioning services. Our staff works together as a team to tackle complex projects and find and target the right bidders for clients' properties.

We operate corporate offices in AL, CA, FL, KY, ME, PA, TX and VA, and the reach of our network of independently owned and operated member companies extends to virtually all corners of the U.S. We offer coast-to-coast services that enhance our reputation as one of the most trusted names in the professional auction industry.

Throughout our decades of business, we've worked with Fortune 500 companies, and multinational institutions as well as private individuals and investors to offer leading innovative solutions for buying and selling commercial and residential real estate. Our unmatched expertise and proven-effective Market-Making System™ allow us to create the best solutions for our clients. Through this innovative methodology, we've helped thousands of home and business owners, financial institutions, and investors accelerate their property sales and get the best return for their assets.
